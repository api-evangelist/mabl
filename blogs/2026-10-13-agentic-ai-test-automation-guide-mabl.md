Key Takeaways Agentic AI test automation helps teams move from isolated task automation to testing workflows that support planning, execution, adaptation, analysis, and human review. The strongest agentic workflows help teams manage coverage, maintenance, visibility, and release confidence as applications change. AI-assisted test creation can help teams move faster, but enterprise testing still needs independent verification, governance, and clear quality signals.
