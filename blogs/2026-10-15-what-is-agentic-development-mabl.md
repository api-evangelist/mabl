Key Takeaways Agentic development provides AI agents with context, tools, and permissions to complete multi-step software tasks under human oversight. Agents can connect planning, implementation, testing, feedback, and review within a coordinated development workflow. Broader agent access increases the risks of quality, security, and accountability, requiring clear controls and approval paths.
