Key Takeaways Regulators don't audit your tests; they audit your evidence, looking for who changed what, when it happened, how it was verified, and who approved it. Compliance isn't earned during an audit; it is a validated state you must maintain through every single release, patch, and configuration change. GxP represents the strictest version of this problem, with FDA data integrity enforcement demonstrating exactly where manual testing evidence inevitably breaks down.
